# Problem Statement

## 1. Title
Agricultural Equipment Rental Marketplace

## 2. Domain
Agriculture / E-Commerce / Rental Management System

## 3. Who is the user? (2–3 user types, with roles)
- Farmers – Search, book, and rent agricultural equipment.
- Equipment Owners – List equipment, manage availability, and approve rental requests.
- Admin – Manage users, equipment listings, bookings, and platform activities.

## 4. What problem are we solving? (3–5 sentences, real-life example)
Many small and medium-scale farmers cannot afford to purchase expensive agricultural equipment such as tractors, harvesters, and tillers. At the same time, many equipment owners have machinery that remains unused for long periods. This results in higher farming costs and inefficient utilization of resources. For example, a farmer who needs a tractor for only two days must either spend a large amount to buy one or struggle to find a nearby rental service. This platform connects farmers with equipment owners, making rentals simple, affordable, and accessible.

## 5. Proposed Solution
The application will provide an online marketplace where equipment owners can register and list their agricultural machinery with pricing and availability. Farmers can search equipment by location and category, book rentals, make payments, and track booking status. The system will also include user authentication, booking management, ratings, and an admin dashboard.

## 6. Core Entities / Database Tables (minimum 5)
- Users
- Equipment
- Categories
- Bookings
- Payments
- Reviews
- Locations

## 7. User Roles & Permissions
- Admin – Manage users, equipment, bookings, and platform operations.
- Equipment Owner – Add, update, and remove equipment; approve or reject rental requests.
- Farmer – Search, book, pay for rentals, and submit reviews.

## 8. Success Criteria
- A farmer should be able to search and book equipment within 2 minutes.
- Equipment owners should be able to list new equipment in under 5 minutes.
- The system should accurately manage booking availability and prevent double bookings.

## 9. Out of Scope
- GPS tracking of equipment.
- IoT-based equipment monitoring.
- Offline rental management.
- Delivery and transportation of equipment.

## 10. Chosen Track
Java (Spring Boot)
